❓ Frequently Asked Questions

Where is Stayzen Hostel located in Indore?

Our flagship property is located at Palasia Square, right near Allen Career Institute, Indore. It is situated in a safe, central residential neighbourhood with quick access to Anand Bazaar, Old Palasia, and Geeta Bhawan.

Is car and two-wheeler parking available?

Yes. Stayzen Hostel features dedicated on-campus four-wheeler car parking as well as secure two-wheeler parking inside our gated premises, monitored 24x7 with CCTV cameras. This provides total convenience for residents and visiting parents.

What meals are included in the mess facility?

We provide 4 fresh, homestyle vegetarian meals every single day: breakfast with tea/milk, nutritious lunch, evening snacks with tea, and wholesome dinner. A weekly rotating menu ensures dietary balance and taste.

How far is Stayzen Hostel from Allen Coaching Palasia?

It is just a 1-minute walk (approx. 100 meters) from Allen Coaching Palasia campus. Students save daily commuting costs and valuable study hours.

What are the security measures and gate closing timings?

The hostel is protected by 24×7 CCTV surveillance, biometric fingerprint access, and a dedicated full-time resident female warden. The main campus gate is safely locked at night, and late entries require prior guardian permission.

How do I book a room or visit the hostel?

Parents and students are welcome to visit any day between 9:00 AM and 7:00 PM. Call us at +91 7024473732 or message on WhatsApp to check live room availability and get a personal guided walkthrough.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions about hostels in Indore

Short, direct answers. If your question is not here, just call us.

Which is the best girls hostel in Indore for students?

STAYZEN operates trusted, 100% dedicated girls residences across prime student zones in Indore — including Trishakti Girls Residency in Bhanwarkuan (Bholaram Ustad Marg) and Little Angel Girls Hostel in Geeta Bhawan (Geeta Nagar, near Ajit Club). Both offer 24×7 security, CCTV, biometric/warden care, homestyle mess meals, and quiet study areas.

What is the monthly rent of the hostels in Indore?

Rent includes wholesome homestyle mess meals and all standard facilities:
• Double & Triple Sharing Rooms: ₹3,600 to ₹7,400 / month
• Single Sharing Rooms (Private 1-Bed): ₹8,600 to ₹13,600 / month
(AC and non-AC options available based on room selection). Call or WhatsApp us to verify current slot availability.

Do the hostels provide in-house mess and canteen facilities?

Yes. Every hostel features an in-house kitchen serving fresh, hygienic, pure vegetarian meals daily. Little Angel Girls Hostel also features a scenic open-space rooftop dining canteen for students to eat and relax in fresh air.

What study and library facilities are available?

Along with individual study desks and chairs in each room, Little Angel Girls Hostel offers a dedicated in-house Study Library with private reading cubicles (Belfast House) so students can prepare for competitive exams in total silence.

How is security ensured for girls?

Safety is non-negotiable. Both Trishakti Girls Residency and Little Angel Girls Hostel have round-the-clock CCTV surveillance at all entry points and corridors, on-site lady wardens, visitor entry logbooks, gated security, and emergency contact support on every floor.

Can parents and students visit the hostel before admission?

Yes, daily visits are warmly welcomed between 9:00 AM and 8:00 PM. Parents and students can inspect the rooms, washrooms, mess kitchen, and study library in person. Contact us via Call or WhatsApp to schedule a viewing slot.
