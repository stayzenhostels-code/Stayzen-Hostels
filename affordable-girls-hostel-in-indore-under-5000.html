The Hidden Costs of Unorganized Cheap PGs in Indore

When searching for budget accommodation, be cautious of unverified landlords who advertise deceptively low headline rents (such as ₹3,000/month) only to hit students with unexpected charges later:

  • Middleman Brokerage Fees: Local agents frequently demand 15 to 30 days rent simply for showing you an address. Always choose a girls pg in indore without brokerage direct owner to save ₹3,000 to ₹5,000 on day one.
  • Separate Mess Subscriptions: Many cheap PGs do not include food. Subscribing to an outside dabba or tiffin service costs an extra ₹2,500 to ₹3,500/month for food that is often cold, oily, and low in protein.
  • Electricity & Water Surcharges: Hidden monthly charges for motor pumps, water coolers, or inflated commercial unit rates.
  • Unfair Security Deposit Deductions: Dishonest caretakers who invent arbitrary 'maintenance' or 'white-wash' fees when you vacate, refusing to return your refundable deposit.
